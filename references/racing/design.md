# Racing adaptation — 2026-10-26

## Reference research

Gameloft's official account of Asphalt 8 describes multiple routes, extreme jumps, spins/barrel rolls that refill nitro, and varied race modes: https://www.gameloft.com/blog/players/asphalt-8-the-high-octane-arcade-racer (accessed 2026-10-26). These are design references, not copied tracks, artwork, branding or claims about current vehicle counts. We adapt the acceleration → corner/drift → jump → earn boost → accelerate loop.

## Original direction

**Meridian / Against Gravity** is a coastal aerospace proving circuit at dusk. A silver Roadster competes against five AI drivers over two laps. An illuminated overhanging gravity ramp is the signature optional shortcut: commit to the center launch lane at speed, climb the original curve, rotate away, transfer sideways on cold gas and land on the return straight. A road bypass preserves a safe alternative. Regular launch wedges, a covered technical sector and an elevated coastal bridge vary the pace.

Auto-acceleration lets players focus on steering, drifting and boost, as in a mobile arcade racer. A/D or arrows steer; Shift drifts/brakes; Space boosts. Mobile has real touch controls. Aerial steering trims the landing and boost stabilizes the transfer. Successful landings and drifts replenish charge. Barrier contact loses speed; rivals use the same speed/charge limits and can make mistakes. No invisible leader teleportation.

Race flow: presentation → 3/2/1/GO → two-lap race → ordered results → retry. A minimap and relative race positions make rivals trackable. Pause, reset, local best time, sound toggle and accessible controls are included. This iteration targets one complete circuit; a career, extra vehicle roster and online multiplayer are future scope, not buttons pretending to work.

## Acceptance targets

- The original film files remain unchanged, recoverable through the baseline tag.
- Real keyboard/touch input starts and controls a moving race; pause freezes it.
- Nitro is finite and drifts/landings restore it. Rivals finish and standings are based on actual progress/time.
- The preserved gravity ramp is visible and traversable, with a road bypass. Landings can be good or costly.
- The route has curves, elevation, a covered sector, and distinct coastal/spaceport vistas.
- Browser checks catch failed resources and exceptions. Selected 404 props retain verifier evidence. Frame-rate readings in software-rendered tests are not physical-device performance claims.
