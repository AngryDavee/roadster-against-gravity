# Directed transfer revision

The first playable build is preserved under `racing-baseline-2026-10-26`. The original film remains unchanged.

The source film shows four downward cold-gas streams, a short attitude-jet burst after departure, ground vapor before wheel contact, and a lingering cloud after drive-away. See the archived source event anchors for actual frame ranges. The game adapts this staging to a continuous race.

The visible ramp and vehicle use the same centerline specification: the source vertical curl plus a 100m lateral sweep completed on the deck. Launch yaw already faces the return lane. No automatic X correction is applied in flight. Player steering remains a real lateral offset, and that offset carries through touchdown. Forward motion brakes into the low hover; downward gas uses a continuous vertical braking curve. Boost shortens the assisted traversal and consumes charge. This is an arcade path model, not a full rocket dynamics simulation.

Camera sequence: wide ramp reveal, closer side view of jet-assisted descent with the upper ramp still visible, then a blended return to chase after touchdown. C or Camera toggles continuous chase. The full reveal fits ramp and landing bounds to the viewport aspect ratio. Landing markings identify the receiving lane without relying on HUD instructions.

Atlas supplied the structural reference and independently reviewed a four-panel source comparison. The first review rated car legibility a qualified pass and geography/gas staging partial. Corrections broadened and softened jet ends, concentrated gas around the descending vehicle by braking forward motion, retained the ramp lip in the landing shot, and added touchdown markings. Do not claim an exact video match or a blanket visual-quality pass.

`evidence/racing/transfer/transfer-preview.mp4` records the actual browser from a deliberately staged approach to the ramp, through its real-time traversal and return to driving. It is a functional visual preview, not an unassisted full race or a performance benchmark. The full-input race evidence is in `evidence/racing/playtest/`.

The second Atlas review remained partial: it identified residual cloud occlusion, limited vertical billowing and ambiguity in the cropped landing view. A final correction increased vertical billowing/cyan edge color while reducing vapor opacity close to the grounded player, retaining the forward body silhouette. The review did not certify exact source likeness. Hover and final wheel-contact frames depict different phases; the source study records wheel contact as partly obscured too.
